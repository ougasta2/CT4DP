\section{Conclusion}
For RQ1, the autumn-evening rule identified a higher large-error rate on the
2024 Ridge forecasts, while adding cluster 0 to the afternoon condition did
not identify a higher rate in that period. For RQ2, the autumn-evening association persisted into 2024 for Ridge, Lasso
and gradient boosting, providing partial evidence of cross-model transfer,
whereas the selected cluster rule did not show comparable persistence. Lasso
improved aggregate accuracy over Ridge in both years, but its errors closely
track Ridge's, so it adds little independent evidence of transfer.
