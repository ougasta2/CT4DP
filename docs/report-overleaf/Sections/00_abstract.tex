\begin{abstract}
We study when day-ahead electricity forecasts incur large errors in
western Denmark (DK1). We fit five models initially on usable
2019--2022 data, then refit monthly on expanding history to score the
same 17,544 delivery hours in 2023--2024. K-means identifies daily
price and weather regimes, and Apriori selects two error-associated
rules from 2023 Ridge forecasts.
When applied unchanged in 2024, autumn evenings had lift 1.77--2.54
across the five models. The cluster-0 afternoon rule had lift 0.86--1.02.
A moving-block interval for the Ridge afternoon comparison also spanned
zero. The results identify autumn evenings as a candidate for targeted
error monitoring and show limited transfer for the selected cluster rule.
\end{abstract}
