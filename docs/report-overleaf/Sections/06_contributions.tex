\section{Individual Contributions}

Naoto Ogawa designed the DK1 forecasting benchmark and assembled the shared dataset. He
aligned five market price series and archived weather forecasts to Copenhagen delivery days,
defined the nominal 10:00 information cutoff, handled daylight-saving transitions, and fixed
the monthly walk-forward evaluation, metrics, and prediction format. He implemented and ran
the Ridge baseline across all 24 monthly folds and verified that the distributed snapshot
reproduced its 17,544 hourly forecasts.

He integrated the K-means labels with the Ridge error series for the comparison of
calendar-only and cluster-containing Apriori rules. He verified that the rules and error
threshold selected in 2023 were held fixed for the 2024 evaluation. He defined the four model
assignments, prepared the independent repositories and English experiment instructions, and
checked that all four starter models scored the same 744 hours.
