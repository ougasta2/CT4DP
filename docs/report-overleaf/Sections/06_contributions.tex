\section{Individual Contributions}
\textbf{Naoto Ogawa} assembled five market price series and archived
weather forecasts, aligned them to Copenhagen delivery days, and defined
the nominal issue time, daylight-saving treatment, and common scoring
protocol. He implemented Ridge, verified its reproduction from the
shared snapshot, and integrated the K-means labels with Ridge errors
for the calendar and cluster rule comparisons. He coordinated model
assignments and aligned predictions for the full cross-model analysis.

\textbf{Xingjie Huang} implemented Lasso and selected its regularization
by rolling 2023 RMSE. He checked convergence and investigated sparsity,
retained features, negative-price errors, and runtime relative to
Ridge. He wrote the Lasso notebook and report sections, supplied
aligned predictions, and revised the full report for consistency.

\textbf{Cheong Shao Hong Justin} ran the Random Forest
benchmark and evaluated leaf sizes on 2022 data. He compared
cluster sizes and Davies--Bouldin scores for alternative values of
\(k\), tested how forest errors changed across \(k=4\) and \(k=5\),
and supplied regime labels for Apriori. He wrote the forest and
clustering notebook and report sections.

\textbf{Fu Nukunu Philip} benchmarked gradient boosting and three
one-setting variants, reran the comparison with pinned dependencies,
and analyzed errors under the fixed and newly mined rules. He
evaluated the afternoon contrast with day blocks and wrote the
experiment, summary, and rule-scoring scripts, the notebook,
and the boosting report sections.

\textbf{Jingruo Xu} benchmarked the MLP starter and built the rolling
validation harness. She tuned width, penalty, and iteration budget on
2023 months, checked sensitivity to the convergence warning, and
compared the tuned forecasts with Ridge by month and clock hour.
She wrote the MLP notebook, scripts, and report sections.
